# Rekord

> Rekord is a Dublin-based fintech platform that helps banks and fintechs automate credit underwriting and customer onboarding. Its AI-native decisioning platform unifies data from multiple sources, orchestrates decision workflows, runs a natural-language policy engine, and provides AI case management so credit and risk teams can make defensible, transparent decisions at scale. Rekord is a portfolio company of point-nine. No public developer API or documentation surface is published at this time.
